I'm interested in **reliable machine learning for time-series data**, especially learning with noisy labels and weak supervision. My broader interests include probabilistic forecasting, deep generative models, and reinforcement learning.

### 🔬 Current research

- **Learning with noisy labels:** Investigating robust time-series classification under imperfect annotations, including human-in-the-loop weak supervision and encoder-based label-refinement approaches.
- **Time-series forecasting:** Collaborating on a research toolkit for exploring encoder–generator architectures and evaluating forecasting models.

### 🧪 Selected projects

- **[TimeGrad — Probabilistic Time-Series Forecasting](https://github.com/AmirHesamKamalpour/TimeGrad-PyTorch-Collaborative-Research-Project)**  
  Co-developed a PyTorch reproduction of an autoregressive diffusion model for multivariate forecasting, with evaluations across five benchmark datasets. *Collaborative project.*

- **[Competitive Dynamic Pricing with Soft Actor-Critic](https://github.com/AmirHesamKamalpour/Competitive-Dynamic-Pricing-RL)**  
  Co-developed a reinforcement learning agent for continuous pricing in a stochastic environment with changing demand and reactive competitors. *Collaborative project.*

- **[TurtleBot3 Global Localization with Particle Filters](https://github.com/Aaidaro/TurtleBot3-Global-Localization-with-Particle-Filters)**  
  Implemented and compared basic, adaptive, and MCMC particle filters for robot localization using ROS 2 and Gazebo.

- **[Multilingual Speech Language Identification](https://github.com/Aaidaro/multilingual-speech-language-identification)**  
  Co-developed a speech classification pipeline combining acoustic feature engineering, supervised learning, and unsupervised clustering.
